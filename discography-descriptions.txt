01. Retrocomtemporary
Album | Released 2025-10-07

Description:
The album where all distortion is suddenly gone, and the audience is greet with clear and soft synthesizer sounds. I experimented with many minimalist composing techniques, introducing variations with addition of elements many automations instead of using a verse-chorous-verse structure. Disc 1 of this album is also considered as my turning-away from adopting solely my personal style, instead, it included many elements from comtemporary electronic music. Disc 2 is a continuation of my former style, the "retro", best represented by "Plumbium".

02. Earlier
Collection | Released 2024-12-31

Description:
As the cover shows, Earlier is a collection of unreleased singles, edits, or "B-sides" from 2020 to 2024. The first six songs except for "Oh." are my absolute earilest works composed using Garageband and mostly improvisation on a MIDI keyboard. They mark some of my happiest memories in the transition from a child to a teenager; among them, "The Universe" and "π" stands out, which I created together with a friend who, just like me, were keen in science across many disciplines. Later in the list, songs 7~9 are the products of a transition period embedded in the making of Expression and Emotion when I kinda got lost in which direction to develope my musical style, and songs 10~11 are unreleased B-sides between Expression and Emotion and Resonance from the Distance.

Tracklist:
1. Difficult (Single)
2. As Bright as Sun (Antarctica Edit)
3. Oh. (Before Tutorial Version)
4. The Universe
5. π
6. Strange Fluid
7. S.S.T.U.P
8. The Gambler
9. Always Keep Your Momentum
10. Forsuge
11. Supernova Countdown

04. Resonance from the Distance
Album | Released 2024-08-23

Description:
The massive use of distortion on almost every synthesizer, instrument, and samples used characterize of this album. At the same time, my very own musical genre "Resonance Electronica" is most overtly manifested in this album. Layering synth plucks with filter envelopes and resonance, an almost iconic sound effect is created and used throughout the album.

05. Expression and Emotion
Album | Released 2023-07-07

Description:
Definitely an emotional outlet for my 13-year-old self. I may be a late boomer to many, and at the time, little to no thought exchange with peers since there's no one to share them with. Thoughts specified by the names of some of those songs are preserved, and emotion was expressed with this album. The music lies in a transition, for I kept on fumbling the mixing and sound designing techniques in FL Studio by myself, but not actually manged to apply them well. It's definitely smoother than 2048, but not as delicate as Resonance from the Distance.

1. Sense
2. Reimpromptu
3. U.W.C
4. 5 Disadvantages of Wearing Revealing Clothes
5. Misallocation
6. Placekeeper
7. We Want Our Money Back

07. 2048
Album | Released 2021-07-15

Description:
The first album made with FL Studio, and of course, with not much knowledge and techniques in mixing and sound design. Starting off with little idea of using such a powerful tool, I focused more on the music itself rather than polishing my mixing. The album is represented by its stark yet complicated 16th drum patterns, my "iconic" Ogun riff, and the occasional use of microtonal pitch shifts.

Tracklist:
1. Oh.
2. Distorting D
3. Electrolysis
4. Pandorium
5. Untitled
6. A Drive into Town Blade for AA12
7. Just Free
8. Real Historical Commemoration

08. The God Wants Me
Album | Released 2020-12-16

Description:
The name of this album is set by a 11-year-old me not yet proficient in English ("want" is the same meaning to that in "wanted"), it's alternate title "Neon Light Stray" may better display the meaning of this album, in which I pictured a very unlucky poor man living a vagrant life in a metropolitan city. The use of soft and dreamy synthesizers from Garageband constructed this particular atmostphere, best represented in UnsubscribeToDream #5.
