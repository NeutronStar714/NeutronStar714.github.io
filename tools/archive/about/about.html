About

NeutronStar714

I am a high-school student who composes, produces, programs and photographs. I am applying to study chemistry and music technology.

Chemistry and music technology

Chemistry is my primary academic subject. Music technology is the second thing I want to study, and it is the half of my work that can be shown rather than described — finished releases, running software, and images.

Most of what is on this site I made on my own: the electronic releases are written, performed, mixed and produced by me, and the plugins are programmed by me in C++ with JUCE. The rock songs are the exception, because those are a band effort.

Electronic music

I began composing in GarageBand at eleven, improvising on a MIDI keyboard and learning the software as I went. My first album, The God Wants Me, came out of that period in December 2020. 2048, the following year, was the first album I made in FL Studio.

Each album since has been a deliberate step. Expression and Emotion (2023) was written while I was teaching myself mixing and sound design. Resonance from the Distance (2024) is where the sound I call Resonance Electronica is clearest — synth plucks layered with filter envelopes and resonances, under heavy distortion. Supercritical Lullaby was my first attempt at house music, and Retrocomtemporary (2025) is a double album that leaves distortion behind on disc one and returns to the retro style on disc two.

The Earlier collection gathers unreleased singles and B-sides from 2020 to 2024, among them “The Universe” and “π”, which I wrote with a friend who was as interested in science as I was.

Rock music, with G10

G10 is my school band. Together we have written and recorded two original songs, which I recorded, mixed and produced with my bandmates.

Audio software

I build my own audio software. The four plugins I have finished are VST3 effects written in C++ with JUCE, and each one begins with an idea of my own rather than a recreation of a classic effect.

Dynamic Resonator, the first to be written up for this site, is a four-band effect that reads the envelope of the incoming signal and moves each filter's frequency in response — upward or downward, at a sensitivity set per band, with its own attack and release.

Photography

Photography is the newest of the four. I photograph hiking trips, highways and cities, and the interest is compositional rather than documentary: I am after images that say something about a place, not only images that record it.
