Editor options

Archiving disables this local editor and preserves your photographs.

Photography editor

Regions contain named locations. Each photograph has its own anonymous coordinates. The editor stays on your computer and is excluded from the published site.

Map

Select what you want to move, then click the map. Moving a region or location changes its anchor; photograph coordinates stay where you put them.

Regions and locations

Delete this region or location

Photographs and coordinates are preserved. Locations return their photographs to the parent region. To delete a region with content, choose another region to receive it.

Photographs

Add photographs up to 30 MB each. JPEG (including camera MPO), PNG, WebP, TIFF, AVIF, BMP, and JPEG 2000 are supported. Choose a region and optionally a named location. GPS supplies each photograph’s anonymous coordinates; missing GPS can be placed manually or left unlocated.
